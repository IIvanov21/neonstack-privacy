Privacy Policy for Neon Stack

Effective date: 5 November 2026
Publisher: IFO Productions
Contact: ifodevproductions@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the game Neon Stack ("the app") handles.

The short version

  • You do not create an account, and the app does not ask for your name, email address or location.
  • Your scores are shared on a public leaderboard under an automatically generated player name, or the name you choose.
  • The app shows ads, which may use your device's advertising ID, subject to your consent where the law requires it.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information the app handles

1. Gameplay data stored on your device. Best scores, settings, the equipped colour theme and the items you own
(such as continue tokens) are saved on your device so the game remembers them.

2. Leaderboard data (Unity Gaming Services). To show world rankings, the app signs you in anonymously with
Unity Authentication, which creates a random player ID. When you finish a run, your score, your player ID, your
display name (a generated name, or the one you choose in the app) and your cosmetic choices (for example a gold name)
are sent to Unity Leaderboards and shown to other players. Do not use a name that identifies you personally.
Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. Advertising (Google AdMob). The app shows banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads through Google AdMob.
AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to
serve and measure ads. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK) the app asks for your
consent before ads are loaded, and you can change your choice later from the Shop screen ("Privacy settings").
You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See Google's policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you buy "Remove ads", the app stops requesting ads.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing). In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. The app receives only the
confirmation of what you bought so it can unlock it, and it does not receive your payment card or account details.

How the information is used

To run the game and its leaderboards, to show and measure ads, to deliver the items you buy, and to keep the app
working and secure.

Sharing

The information above is shared only with the service providers named in this policy (Unity and Google) so that
the features work. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Leaderboard entries are tied to a random player ID
and remain on the leaderboard until they are removed or the leaderboard is reset. To ask for your leaderboard data to
be deleted, contact us at the address above and include your player name.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent: Shop screen, "Privacy settings" (shown where consent applies).
  • Reset your advertising ID: Android Settings, Privacy, Ads.
  • Stop all ads: buy "Remove ads" in the Shop.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
